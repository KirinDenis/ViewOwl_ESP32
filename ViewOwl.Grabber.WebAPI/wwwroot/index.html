Flashing requires Chrome or Edge — these are the only browsers that support Web Serial (USB access). Firefox and Safari cannot flash devices from the browser.

Connect your ESP32 via a data-capable USB cable. No additional drivers are needed on Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, or most Linux systems. Click ▶ Flash to Device, pick your display model, and both the firmware and your chosen template transfer automatically in about 30 seconds.
